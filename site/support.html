Support

Last updated 6 October 2026

Need help? The fastest way is in the app: Settings → Help. Send us a message and we’ll reply right there. You can also email nextlevelsport.app@gmail.com.

Common questions

How do I join my team?

Ask your coach for your team’s 6-character join code (like HQ7-4KD). Open your sport, tap “With a team”, enter the code and tap Ask to Join. Your coach then accepts you.

I’m a coach — how do I set up my team?

Open your sport and tap “I’m a coach” (or change your role in Settings → Account Settings → Your Role). Create your team and you’ll get a join code to give your players. Accept their requests on your Coach Dashboard.

What can my coach see?

Only the logs you mark “My team”. Your “Just me” logs and your videos are private to you.

I forgot my password

On the Log In screen, type your email and tap “Forgot password?”. We’ll email you a link to set a new one (check your junk folder).

Where are my videos?

Videos are saved only on the phone you added them from, so they won’t appear on another device. Adding the app to your Home Screen helps your phone keep them safe.

How do I report a team or coach?

Go to Settings → Account Settings → Your Team and tap “Report this team or coach”. It comes straight to us.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account Settings → Delete Account and confirm with your password. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it, in every sport.

My account says “suspended”

Accounts are suspended if they break our Terms. If you think it’s a mistake, email nextlevelsport.app@gmail.com with your username.
