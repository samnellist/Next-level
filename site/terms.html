You need to be 13 or over. You’re responsible for your account, so keep your password safe and don’t share your login.

Only give your team’s join code to players you actually coach, and only accept players you know. Use what players share with you only to help them improve. You’re responsible for following your club’s and sport’s safeguarding rules when working with young people.

Health and safety

Drills, training plans, nutrition ideas, Coach Bot answers and video technique analysis are general guidance to help you improve. They are not medical, physiotherapy or professional coaching advice. Warm up properly, train within your limits, and stop if something hurts. If you have an injury or health condition, speak to a doctor, physio or qualified coach before changing your training or diet.

What you log is yours. You let us store and process it only to run the app for you (and to share it with your coach when you choose “My team”). Videos stay on your device.

If someone reports a team or coach, we’ll look into it. We may remove teams or suspend accounts that break these terms or put others at risk. If you think we’ve made a mistake, contact us through the Support page.

Paid plans and free trial

Every plan starts with a 1-month free trial. You set up the subscription through PayPal before the trial starts, but nothing is taken during the trial. If you don't cancel before the trial ends, PayPal takes your first payment then, and again every month or year until you cancel.

Cancelling: go to Settings → Account Settings → Subscription → Cancel subscription, and confirm in PayPal (Settings → Payments → Manage automatic payments). Cancel within the 1-month trial and you pay nothing. If you cancel after paying, you won't be charged again; we don't refund the part of a month or year already paid, except where the law says we must. If a payment fails, we may pause access until it's sorted.
