Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

The short version: your logs are private to you. Your coach only sees logs you mark “My team”. Videos never leave your phone. We don’t sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Settings.

Who we are

Next Level Sport (“Next Level”, “we”) is run by Sam Nellist. We decide how your information is used. You can contact us about privacy at nextlevelsport.app@gmail.com or through Help in the app.

What we collect

  • Your account: username, email address, nationality and age. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication — we never see it.
  • Your sport settings: for each sport, whether you’re a player or coach and your main position.
  • What you log: matches, rounds and training sessions and their stats, self-ratings, conditions, opponents or courses, seasons, goals, gym workouts, nutrition entries, notes, and your Coach Bot chat history.
  • Teams: the teams you coach or join, join requests, and which logs you choose to share with your coach.
  • Messages to us: help messages you send and any reports you make about a team or coach.

Videos

Videos you add are stored only on your own device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else, and your coach can’t see them. Technique analysis also runs on your device. The first time you use it, the app downloads Google’s free body-tracking tool (MediaPipe) — no video or picture of you is sent anywhere.

Payments

Subscriptions are paid through PayPal. Your card and PayPal details are entered on PayPal and held by PayPal — Next Level never sees or stores them. We keep only what we need to know your plan: the plan you chose, monthly or yearly, your trial and payment dates, and PayPal's reference number for your subscription. If you cancel, we delete the dates and email from that record straight away, and delete the rest once the subscription is stopped in PayPal. PayPal's own privacy notice covers what they hold.

Who can see your information

  • You can see everything you’ve logged.
  • Your coach can see your username, position and only the logs you mark “My team” while you’re in their team. They never see your “Just me” logs, videos or account details.
  • The Next Level team can see usernames, each person’s role and position per sport, team details, help messages and reports, so we can support you and keep the app safe. We do not look at your private logs.
  • Our service providers: Google Firebase (database and sign-in) and Netlify (which hosts the website). They process data only to run the service for us.

We never sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Where it’s stored

Your logs and account data are stored in Google Firebase’s London (europe-west2) data centre. Firebase Authentication and Netlify may process some data (such as sign-in details or your device’s IP address for security) outside the UK, under the safeguards those companies use for international transfers. The app also saves some information on your device so it can work offline.

Why we use it

We use your information to provide the app you’ve signed up for — saving your logs, analysing them, giving you drills, and letting you share with your coach if you choose. We use reports and help messages to support you and to keep the app safe, for example by removing fake teams or suspending accounts that break our Terms.

Children

You need to be 13 or over to use Next Level. If you’re under 18, we recommend a parent or guardian knows you’re using it. Accounts are private by default and there are no messages between players and coaches.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account (Settings → Account Settings → Delete Account), your account, logs, teams you coach, help messages and reports are deleted straight away, along with videos on that device.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it. Most of this you can do yourself in the app. For anything else, contact nextlevelsport.app@gmail.com. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
