▶ Clip HQ
To use "Save to Photos" (one-time setup):
  1. Tap here to download the Clip HQ certificate, then tap Allow.
  2. Open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → "Clip HQ Local CA" → Install.
  3. Open Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and switch on "Clip HQ Local CA".
  4. Then open the secure link and add it to your Home Screen.
Until then you can still use Download (then tap Share → Save Video).
